Privacy Policy

Computer Art LLC

Effective 23 October 2026

Computer Art LLC ("we", "the studio") is a two-person creative studio based in Boston, Massachusetts. This policy describes how we handle information in connection with our Instagram account (@computerartllc) and the software we use to read activity on it.

What this covers

We operate a small internal tool, registered with Meta as the app "Computer Art LLC inbound", that reads activity on our own Instagram account. It does not operate on anyone else's account and it is not offered to the public.

What we collect

Through Meta's Instagram API, the tool reads:

  • Public comments left on our Instagram posts, including the commenter's username, the comment text, and when it was posted.
  • Direct messages sent to our account, including the sender's username, the message text, and when it was sent.
  • Public profile information that Instagram makes available for professional accounts, such as a username, display name, bio, website, and follower count, when we look up someone who has contacted us.

We do not collect passwords, private account data, or information about anyone who has not interacted with our account.

Why we collect it

We use this information for one purpose: to understand who is contacting the studio and whether to follow up with them about working together. In practice this means reading comments and messages, noting who sent them, and keeping a short record of the ones that are relevant to the studio's work.

How we use and store it

  • Records are kept in private files on studio computers. They are not published, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • The tool is read-only. It never posts, comments, replies, likes, follows, or sends messages on our behalf.
  • We keep records for as long as they are useful for the studio's work and delete them on request.

Sharing

We do not share this information with anyone outside the studio, except where required by law.

Your choices

If you have commented on or messaged our account and would like us to delete any record we hold about you, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

Meta's role

Access to this information is provided by Meta through the Instagram API, under Meta's platform terms and Instagram's own data policy. Meta's handling of your information is governed by Meta's policies, not this one.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
